DOOM console on the FPGA bench, 2026-11-23 (docs/25-DOOM-CONSOLE.md).
Host: a Pico W at 250 MHz running rp2040-doom with the console backend; chip: version 3.1's RTL on the
Tang Primer 25K (chip.fs sha256 6cd77342...), clocked at 25 MHz from the Pico. SSD1306 on uio[2] (SCL) and
uio[3] (SDA); pwm_audio's uio[6] through 1 kohm / 33 nF / 10 kohm / 1.5 kohm into a laptop's headset jack.
The game ran its demo loop. The Pico prints one status line every 2 s (counts since boot) and the zone
low-water line when it falls; 60 s of its serial output, unedited:

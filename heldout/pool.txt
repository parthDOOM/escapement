# Escapement held-out pool (2026-11-04). None of these is in firmware/ as of v1.6.
# Eight are drawn at random from this pool and sealed (sha-256 of salt + list) before any
# of them is written. Each drawn protocol is then written from its public specification,
# certified by escert, co-simulated against an independent model, and run on the FPGA
# bench; every outcome, failures included, is published when the list is opened.
1  MIL-STD-1553B remote terminal (Manchester II, 1 Mbit/s)
2  I2S transmitter (Philips format, 16-bit stereo)
3  SDI-12 sensor (1200 baud, break and marking)
4  MDIO, IEEE 802.3 clause 22 manager
5  ISO 7816-3 smart card reader, T=0, ATR
6  S/PDIF transmitter (IEC 60958, biphase mark)
7  HDLC framing (flags, bit stuffing, CRC-16/X.25)
8  SMBus host with PEC (CRC-8)
9  Microwire, 93C46 EEPROM read and write
10 DMX512 receiver
11 MIDI receiver (31,250 baud, running status)
12 TM1637 LED display driver (two-wire, not I2C)
13 HD44780 LCD, 4-bit parallel interface
14 DHT22 temperature and humidity sensor (one wire)
15 SSI absolute encoder (synchronous serial interface)
16 BiSS-C encoder interface
17 KNX TP1 (9600 bit/s, bit coding and ack)
18 IO-Link device, COM1 (4.8 kbit/s)
19 PS/2 host (keyboard side reversed: the chip is the host)
20 Nintendo 64 / GameCube controller (joybus, 4 us bits)
21 SNES controller (latch and shift)
22 Philips RC-MM infrared
23 Profibus DP framing on RS-485 (11-bit characters)
24 M-Bus (meter bus) slave, EN 13757-2 framing
